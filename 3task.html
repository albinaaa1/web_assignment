Kim Bum

South Korean Actor, Singer & Model

About Kim Bum

Kim Bum (born Kim Sang-bum on July 7, 1989) is a highly acclaimed South Korean actor, model, and singer. He rose to international stardom for his iconic role as So Yi-jung in the hit television drama Boys Over Flowers (2009). Over his career, he has showcased remarkable acting versatility, playing complex roles ranging from a guardian angel in Padam Padam to a half-gumiho in Tale of the Nine Tailed. His dedication to performance and charismatic presence have made him one of the most recognized figures in the Hallyu wave.

Career Timeline & Major Milestones

  1. 1989: Born on July 7 in Seoul, South Korea.
  2. 2006: Made his acting debut in the sitcom High Kick!.
  3. 2009: Achieved international fame with Boys Over Flowers and won the Netizen Popularity Award.
  4. 2011: Starred in the critically acclaimed fantasy drama Padam Padam.
  5. 2020: Made a successful screen return as Lee Rang in Tale of the Nine Tailed.
  6. 2021–2022: Played lead roles in the hit television series Law School and Ghost Doctor.

Key Roles & Achievements

  • Breakthrough role as So Yi-jung in Boys Over Flowers.
  • Acclaimed performance as Han Joon-hwi in the legal drama Law School.
  • Fan-favorite portrayal of the charismatic anti-hero Lee Rang in Tale of the Nine Tailed.
  • Demonstrated impressive medical comedic timing as Go Seung-tak in Ghost Doctor.
  • Active contribution to global charity campaigns and Korean Wave (Hallyu) promotion worldwide.
